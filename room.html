S07
Product law: how trust is earned

For a regulated institution this is the first slide, not the last.

Policy and architecture require a human gate before any agent acts on money in any amount, anything irreversible, or any external action above a de-minimis line. No fee or tier is designed to purchase an exception.
Autonomy is earned per decision class, never assumed. An agent graduates only after matching the human's actual choice 90% of the time across 20 or more repetitions, with executive sign-off, and a single disagreement demotes it back to a full human gate.
The system is designed to fail open to surfacing. Decisions, money, deadlines, and irreversible items are designed so they cannot be silently suppressed.
Governance designed to anticipate supervisory expectations. The control loop maps onto NIST AI RMF (Govern, Map, Measure, Manage), anticipates established model-risk-management supervisory expectations, and aligns with NYDFS-style cyber expectations SURVEY framework sources, 2026. The audit trail is the evidence, not a slogan.
Every vendor says "human in the loop." Almost none can show you the graduation ledger. We can, and for a crypto-custody bank that auditable difference is the whole conversation.
S08
Reward, not replace

Every employee has heard the quiet threat inside "AI transformation." This system inverts it.

Adoption earns recognition, standing, and time back. This program is designed so adoption increases an employee's value, and we structure the engagement to that end. Contribution is scored so that value is visible, and the scoring is built to protect people from the failure modes scoring systems always have.

  • Reward with consent, never surveillance. Adoption is never mandated; anyone can mark work complete manually and the system still works. The game rewards the willing and never punishes the rest.
  • Analytics are backend and legal-gated. Bottleneck and adoption data help the org find where work stalls, held behind legal review before any of it informs a person-level decision, with no data path from scores to compensation.
Meta's internal AI-token leaderboard, 85,000+ employees, was shut down after press exposure VENDOR Fortune 2026-04-09. Surveillance with points fails. Reward with consent is the opposite design, and it is the one we build.
S09
The premise

Let's say the uncomfortable part ourselves: the frontier labs can build this.

That is not a risk to this plan. It is the premise of the market. Capability does not install itself. A company needs someone who knows its people, its meetings, its objectives, and its risk lines, and who stays accountable while the org changes underneath. That is a companion role, not a platform feature.

  • The premise is now visibly true: Anthropic launched a $1.5B enterprise AI services joint venture with Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman, and Goldman Sachs VENDOR company announcement + CNBC, 2026-05-04. The labs themselves are entering the transition motion.
  • Hygiene, in one line: the system can swap the model vendor it runs on, and every account and key is audited. A hedge, not a fear posture.
The labs build the capability. Someone still has to walk Anchorage through the transition. We are that companion, and we hand you a head start your competitors have to earn later, at a worse time.
